# Source Notes

## Home Office asylum support

The Home Office publishes asylum seekers in receipt of support by local authority in Asy_D11. This includes supported asylum seekers, not all asylum seekers and not all people without lawful immigration status.

## Nationality profile

The nationality profile used in this repository is national asylum-claimant data, not local-authority-level ethnicity data.

## Crime rates

The crime-rate graphics use local recorded crime-rate context, typically crimes per 1,000 residents, from Police.uk / ONS local crime comparison tables.

## Data limitations

Area-level co-location is not causation. Local crime rates do not identify the immigration status of offenders.
